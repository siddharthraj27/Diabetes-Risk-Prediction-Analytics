DISEASE RISK PREDICTION AND PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
Individual Data Analytics Mini-Project

OBJECTIVE
Explore the Early Stage Diabetes Risk Prediction dataset, train Logistic Regression, and evaluate classification performance.

DATASET
Early Stage Diabetes Risk Prediction dataset from UCI.
Original records: 520; features: 16; target: class.
Dataset source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/529/early+stage+diabetes+risk+prediction+dataset

METHODOLOGY
1. Load and inspect dataset.
2. Remove duplicate rows and trim text.
3. Encode categorical columns.
4. Visualize class distribution, symptoms, age and correlations.
5. Stratified 80/20 train/test split (random_state=42).
6. Train Logistic Regression.
7. Evaluate with confusion matrix and standard metrics.
